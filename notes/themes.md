# Themes and Questions

## Recovery

Recovery is not just abstinence. The novel asks what is supposed to replace intoxication once the crisis is over.

## Ordinary consciousness

The protagonist's deepest problem is not simply drug craving. It is the suspicion that ordinary consciousness does not generate enough salience to compete with intoxication.

## Boredom

Possible thesis:

**Boredom is the negative feedback mechanism by which consciousness searches the possibility space.**

If so, removing boredom may reduce suffering while also altering agency.

## Authenticity

If a device changes what a person finds meaningful, are the resulting preferences less authentic than preferences shaped by childhood, culture, medication, trauma, habit, or recovery?

## Freedom

If a person's relapse can be predicted from neural and behavioral data before they consciously decide to use, what does that imply about choice?

## Meaning

Science may be able to change craving, salience, reward prediction, memory, and subjective time.

It cannot answer:

**What should I want?**
