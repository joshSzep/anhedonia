# Title and Cover

## Title

**Anhedonia**

The title works because the protagonist's condition is not straightforward anhedonia. He can still experience pleasure. His deeper problem is sustained engagement, salience, and the sense that ordinary experience is worth remaining present for.

The novel can gradually interrogate whether "anhedonia" is even the correct diagnosis.
